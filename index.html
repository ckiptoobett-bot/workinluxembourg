Application Follow-Up & Assessment Process

Shortlisting Notification: Applicants who meet the initial selection criteria will receive an official notification via email within 72 hours of submitting their applications. Notifications will be sent from luxemburgjobopportunities2026@gmail.com. Applicants are encouraged to monitor their inbox and spam/junk folders regularly.

Assessment Requirements: Shortlisted candidates will be required to complete a two-part assessment designed to evaluate their Language Proficiency and Professional Specialisation in their respective fields.

Assessment Fee: A fee of €50 applies for the complete assessment process.

Important Note: Submission of an application or payment of the assessment fee does not guarantee employment, a visa, or a job offer. Final recruitment and visa decisions are made by prospective employers and Luxembourg immigration authorities.
