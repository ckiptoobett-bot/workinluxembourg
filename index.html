About Work in Luxembourg

Empowering international professionals with a data-driven path to the Grand Duchy.

The Talent to Luxembourg initiative was born in 2024 from a simple observation by its Founder, Ruggero Monelli: many talented candidates approach the Luxembourg job market without truly understanding what makes a professional relationship with an employer succeed.

Drawing on years of firsthand experience in the Grand Duchy, Ruggero decided to turn that insight into action — extending an open invitation to professionals from abroad to explore career opportunities and relocate to Luxembourg.

The approach is deliberately data-driven, built on an extensive mapping of the Luxembourg ecosystem.

🎯 Talent & Recruitment

• A curated list of around 100 recruiters based in Luxembourg, complemented by 100 international recruiters based in the UK and beyond.
• A directory of around 1,300 companies offering jobs in Luxembourg, with ready-to-use links to live job offers, organized across approximately 150 industry sectors.
• The complete annual calendar of job fairs in Luxembourg, including detailed profiles of hundreds of employers from past editions.

🌍 Practical Support & Relocation

• Online tools for CV restructuring, job portals, and a full overview of professional certifications relevant to the local market.
• A directory of training providers offering tailored programmes.
• Relocation guides produced by specialized expat organizations, including real estate companies offering rental services.
